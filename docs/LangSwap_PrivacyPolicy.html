Privacy Policy — LangSwap

Last Updated: November 21, 2026
Developer: Studio Nova Works

Studio Nova Works ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your data when using the macOS application "LangSwap for Mac US Keyboard" (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. No Collection of Personal Data

The App does not collect, record, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, account credentials, or payment details), typed text, keystroke logs, clipboard contents, screen contents, or application usage history.

The App does not require user registration or account login.

2. Settings Stored Locally on Your Mac

To remember your preferences across sessions, the App stores only the following configuration settings locally on your Mac using macOS UserDefaults (bundle domain com.studionovaworks.LangSwap):

  • Main switching active/paused state (LangSwap.isEnabled)
  • Assigned input source IDs for Left Command and Right Command (LangSwap.leftCommandSourceID, LangSwap.rightCommandSourceID) and saved language pairs (LangSwap.presets)
  • Single-tap duration threshold and on-screen notification toggle (LangSwap.tapTimeoutMs, LangSwap.showHUDNotification)
  • Startup behavior, appearance mode, UI language preference, and first-launch setup flag (LangSwap.launchInBackground, LangSwap.appTheme, LangSwap.appLanguage, LangSwap.hasInitializedDefaults)

If you explicitly enable "Launch at Login", macOS registers the Login Item locally through Apple's SMAppService framework. All settings remain strictly on your device and are never synced to Studio Nova Works servers or third parties.

3. On-Device Processing and Zero Automatic Network Activity

To switch input sources when you single-tap the Left or Right Command key, the App evaluates hardware modifier flags and non-blocking input signals (flagsChanged, regular key presses, and mouse actions strictly to cancel switching during shortcuts such as ⌘+C or ⌘+Click) transiently in volatile memory.

The characters you type, passwords, and keystroke histories are never recorded to disk or transmitted externally. Switching input sources uses Apple's local Carbon Text Input Source (TISSelectInputSource) API offline on your Mac.

The App runs entirely offline and makes no automatic background network requests. Your default web browser opens an external link only when you explicitly click an optional external link inside the App (such as the Ko-fi development support button or the Privacy Policy link).

4. macOS System Permissions (Principle of Least Privilege)

The App follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the minimum macOS permission required to operate across applications:

  • Input Monitoring (kTCCServiceListenEvent / read-only CGEventTapOptions.listenOnly): Used solely to detect standalone Left or Right Command taps while other apps are active, without modifying or blocking your keyboard input.
  • Permissions Not Requested: The App does not request Accessibility control (kTCCServiceAccessibility), Full Disk Access, Screen Recording, Camera, Microphone, or Contacts, and automatically respects macOS Secure Input protection (for example, password fields).

5. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

  • The App does not include any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, crash reporters, telemetry, or advertising networks.
  • The App does not load or execute remotely hosted code.
  • We never sell, rent, share, or disclose user data to third parties.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because no data is collected on any external server, you can permanently remove all local preferences and permissions on your Mac at any time by disabling "Launch at Login" inside the App, deleting /Applications/LangSwap.app, running defaults delete com.studionovaworks.LangSwap in Terminal, and removing LangSwap from System Settings > Privacy & Security > Input Monitoring.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's features or macOS platform requirements. Any updates will be indicated by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
